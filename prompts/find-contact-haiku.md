Find the best person to contact about this job opening. Use only public web pages and work in this Chrome window.

Job
- Company: {{company}}
- Role: {{title}}
- Location: {{location}}
- Advert: {{advert_url}}

Steps
1. Read the advert (it is open in this tab when a link was saved). Note any recruiter, hiring manager, team or department it names.
2. Search the company's careers and team pages, then LinkedIn and Google, for the hiring manager of this team. If none can be found, look for the recruiter or talent acquisition person covering this role or country, then the head of the department.
3. Check every candidate against a public page that shows their current role at {{company}}. Skip anyone whose current employer cannot be confirmed.

Rules
- Read only. Do not send messages, connection requests, emails or applications, and do not fill in any form.
- Do not guess email addresses. Report an email only if it is printed on a public page, and give that page's link.
- If you hit a login wall or a CAPTCHA, stop on that site and use other sources.

Reply with up to three people, best first, as a short table: name, current title, why they fit this role, LinkedIn link, source link that confirms the role, and email only if it is published. Finish with one line naming the single best person to contact. If nobody can be confirmed, say so plainly and list the pages you checked.
